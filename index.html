The HS&E test itself has 50 questions in 45 minutes, and you need 45 right to pass. To apply for a CSCS card you need to have passed the right HS&E test, or an approved alternative.

Each mock is a new mix of 50 from 310 practice questions, 10 from each of the 5 Operatives topics. At the end you get your score, then the right answer and the reason for every one you got wrong.
