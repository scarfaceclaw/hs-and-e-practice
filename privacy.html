How we use your details

Who we are. HS&E Test Practice is run by Andrew Domakin, a sole trader in the UK. Contact: [business email], [geographic address].

What we collect. Your email address and, if you choose, which test you are taking and a language. We also record the date and time, which version of this notice you saw, and whether you came from our Google ad. We do not ask your name.

Why, and our legal basis.

  • To email you once when the HS&E Test Practice iPhone app is on the App Store. Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by pressing "Email me when it is out". You do not have to: the practice questions and the mock work without it.
  • To count how many people want each test and each language, and how many came from our Google ad, so we can decide what to build and translate first and whether our ad works. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in making those decisions. We only ever use these counts as totals. You can object at any time by writing to [business email].

We do not use your details for anything else.

Who else has your details. Google, which runs Google Forms: it stores the answers in our Google account and also handles them under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Google may store data in the United States; Google LLC is covered by the UK–US data bridge. GitHub, which hosts this page, records visitors' IP addresses for security. We never sell your details or give them to anyone else.

How long we keep them. We delete them after we send the launch email, and by 31 October 2027 at the latest, even if the app is not out by then.

Your rights. You can take back your consent at any time: write to [business email], or reply "stop" to our email. You can ask for a copy of your details, or ask us to correct or delete them. If you are unhappy with how we use your details, tell us: we will confirm we have your complaint within 30 days and then deal with it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk.

Cookies. This page sets no cookies and uses no tracking.

No decisions are made about you by computer.
