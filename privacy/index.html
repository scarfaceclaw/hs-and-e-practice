Privacy policy: the HS&E Test Practice app

This policy is about the HS&E Test Practice iPhone app. Last updated 20 October 2026.

Who we are. The app is made by Andrew Domakin, a sole trader in the UK. Contact: hsande@protonmail.com, 68 Woodland Avenue, Hutton, Brentwood, Essex CM13 1HH.

In short: the app collects no personal data. We cannot see who uses it, what you answer or how you score.

  • No account. There is no sign-up and no log-in. The app never asks your name or email.
  • No server. The app has no server of ours to talk to. The questions are inside the app, and it works with no signal.
  • Your progress stays on your iPhone. Your answers, scores, test choice, help language and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • No adverts, no analytics, no tracking. The app contains no third-party code: no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Read aloud uses your iPhone's own voices, on the phone. Nothing you hear is sent anywhere.

Buying full access. The one-off purchase is made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). We never see your name, card or Apple Account details. Apple sends us sales reports with totals, such as how many purchases were made in each country, which do not identify you. "Restore purchase" asks Apple whether your Apple Account has already bought full access; we see nothing of this.

Links out of the app. The app's About screen links to the Open Government Licence on the National Archives website. If you tap it, that website opens in your browser under its own privacy policy.

Our website and email sign-up. This website is separate from the app. If you gave us your email address on it to hear when the app is out, that is covered by How we use your details.

Children. The app is meant for people preparing for the CITB HS&E test, usually 16 and over. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

If you email us. If you write to hsande@protonmail.com, we use your email and message only to reply to you, and delete them within 12 months of our last reply.

Your rights. Because the app collects nothing, we hold no app data about you. If you have a question or complaint about privacy, write to hsande@protonmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk.

Changes. If the app ever starts to collect data, we will update this page before that version is released, and change the date above.
