Help with the HS&E Test Practice app

Contact us. Email hsande@protonmail.com. We aim to reply within two working days. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what happened.

Restore your purchase

New phone, or reinstalled the app? Your full access is tied to your Apple Account, so you do not pay again.

  • Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • Open the app and tap Get full access on the home screen.
  • Tap Restore purchase.

Full access is a one-off purchase. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com, or contact us and we will help.

Your progress

Your answers and scores are saved only on your iPhone. There is no account, so they do not move to a new phone, and deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.

Booking the real test

This app does not book tests. Book your HS&E test with CITB.
